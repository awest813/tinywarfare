# Asset licenses

## Marine soldier model

`assets/models/source/marine.glb` is the low-poly Marine character supplied by
the project owner for Tiny Warfare (`SoldierCharacter.glb` with its two
embedded camouflage atlases). `tools/build-operatives.mjs` derives the in-game
soldiers from it:

- `assets/models/operative_us.glb` (multicam atlas)
- `assets/models/operative_ru.glb` (grey atlas through a green filter)

Their animation comes from the two Quaternius packs below, retargeted onto
the Marine's rig, plus weapon holds (aim, low ready, sprint carry, reload)
authored in the same tool by inverse kinematics. Check the Marine's original
licence terms before redistributing the source model outside this project.
The GitHub Pages build (`tools/build-pages.mjs`) leaves `assets/models/source/`
and the retired `soldier_*.glb` out: the game loads only the derived
operatives.

## Quaternius Universal Animation Library (Standard)

`assets/models/source/ual_clips.glb` is a trimmed copy (skeleton plus the 14
clips the soldiers use; `tools/extract-ual.mjs`) of the free Standard edition
of Quaternius'
[Universal Animation Library](https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-animation-library),
released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/),
as mirrored at <https://github.com/J-Ponzo/gltf-universal-animation-library>.
The operatives' locomotion (idle, walk, jog, sprint, crouch, jump), hit
reactions and their first death are retargeted from it. Attribution is not
required by CC0, but is included with thanks to Quaternius.

## Quaternius Toon Shooter Game Kit

The following files are derived from the
[Quaternius Toon Shooter Game Kit](https://quaternius.com/packs/toonshootergamekit.html),
which Quaternius released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/):

- `assets/models/soldier_ct.glb` (the source of the operatives' second death,
  and the reference for the body-merge tests; no longer drawn in game)
- `assets/models/soldier_t.glb`
- `assets/models/viewmodels/npc-arms-ct.glb` (a body-stripped derivative of
  `soldier_ct.glb`)

The files were processed and exported to GLB for Tiny Strike. Attribution is
not required by CC0, but is included with thanks to Quaternius.

## Claude-of-Duty (OVERWATCH) rendering code

Everything under `src/gfx/` is ported from
[mshumer/Claude-of-Duty](https://github.com/mshumer/Claude-of-Duty), released
under the [MIT License](https://github.com/mshumer/Claude-of-Duty/blob/main/LICENSE)
(Copyright (c) 2026 mshumer). The MIT notice is reproduced in
[`src/gfx/LICENSE`](src/gfx/LICENSE) and applies to those files.

What was taken, and what changed:

| ported | from | adapted for Tiny Strike |
|---|---|---|
| `src/gfx/materials/` | `src/materials/` | standalone construction (renderer injected, no subsystem registry); two new surfaces — wind-packed snow and ashlar stone — in `glsl/surfaces-extra.js` |
| `src/gfx/sky/` | `src/sky/` | volumetrics not ported; the dome tone maps itself for our LDR pipeline and reads a baked equirect instead of evaluating the atmosphere per pixel; auto-exposure metering added |
| `src/gfx/kit/util.js` | `src/world/util.js` | unchanged geometry toolkit |
| `src/gfx/weapons/{geometry,parts,materials,mathx}.js`, `models/{rifle,smg,pistol}.js` | `src/weapons/` | variant parameters added to the builders; `models/{ak,mp5,sniper,deagle}.js` are new work for this project |

No art assets were copied — the reference project generates every texel and
every triangle procedurally, and so does this one.

## Tiny Strike original assets

The weapon and equipment GLBs in `assets/models/viewmodels/`, excluding the
Quaternius-derived arm file listed above, were authored for Tiny Strike from
the game's original procedural weapon designs. They are released under the
project's [ISC License](LICENSE).

Textures and game audio are generated from project source code. The trailer
soundtrack is generated by `tools/trailer-audio.js` and is also covered by the
project's ISC License.
